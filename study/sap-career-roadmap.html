Review of the ChatGPT plan

  • Checked and correct: SAP's page lists C_TB120 as 2 hours, 60% to pass, scenario-based, and includes the Web client. My earlier B1 plan quoted an older 65% figure from a third party, so trust SAP's page. The question count is not stated there.
  • Time: ChatGPT's 12 months only works at roughly 10 hours a week. With the Coursera courses this plan takes 58 weeks at about 8 hours a week, plus 2 to 3 extra SQL hours a week in weeks 1 to 13. If that is too much, drop the Coursera SQL course and add about 4 weeks to the first phase.
  • Salary figures: the ranges, including $180k to $220k for architects, are ChatGPT's planning targets. I did not verify them or the job ads it cited. Entry-level remote roles are rare, which it also said.
  • Python: ChatGPT itself warned that enterprise SAP jobs lean on BTP, ABAP, IDocs and OData more than Python, but you chose Coursera for the Python, so it now takes about ten weeks, and the SAP-specific weeks still follow it.
  • Database: it assumed SQL Server. Your B1 may run on HANA, so week 1 asks your admin.
  • Left for later: ABAP and Fiori development, which it suggests learning alongside real implementation work.
  • Honesty: keep your resume wording matched to what you actually did, as you said you would. Projects use sample data, never company data or code.
