After the exam

  1. Take the free Processes in SAP S/4HANA EWM course for a second pass on the core flows.
  2. Update your resume and LinkedIn with the EWM certification and your floor-to-system document's lessons, keeping claims honest.
  3. Search for warehouse systems analyst and WM or EWM support roles, and note which ones mention classic WM, since many still do.
  4. Look for chances to work with your company's SAP team, because most of these roles expect hands-on experience beyond the exam.
