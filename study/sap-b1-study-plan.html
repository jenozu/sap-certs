SAP Business One certification plan

Fourteen weeks to the SAP Certified Associate exam for Business One 10.0, built around one hour a day on weekdays, a longer hands-on session on Saturday, and a short review on Sunday. Tick days off on the calendar or in the checklist; both stay in sync on this device.

Checklist

Click a day's title to open its steps, and tick the box when the day is done. Do the steps in order and pace yourself to the time shown. "Journey" means the SAP learning journey for that phase. Menu and lesson names can differ slightly by version and by what your user is allowed to see, so go by what you find on screen. Milestones and bonus tasks sit at the bottom of each week, and bonus tasks are not counted in your progress.

After the exam

  1. Take the free Leveraging the SAP Business One Service Layer course, with Postman.
  2. Ask your admin for a limited API user and a test database.
  3. Turn your "how B1 does it vs how we do it" list into one small, read-only report or time-saver, with ChatGPT writing the code and your admin reviewing it.
  4. Only then try write operations, and only on the test database.

Study time is about 108 hours in total. Lesson names are described by topic, because SAP's journey page order may change, so follow the journey's own sequence. Exam price, format and subscription terms were not confirmed from SAP, so check them on the exam page before you pay.
